Privacy Policy

2026-10-07

CSV Preflight needs no account and processes selected files locally on your Mac. The app implements no file upload, analytics, telemetry, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests.

Selected files are read for inspection. Reports are written only to the destination you choose. Inspection data is processed in memory. File contents are not sent to the developer.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and adds no cookies or analytics. GitHub and Apple operate their services under their own privacy policies. Information you submit for support is used to respond to your request. Do not submit confidential CSV files.
